Privacy Policy

One policy for every Arami Studios app.

THE SHORT VERSION

Our apps don't collect any personal data. No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. Your settings and progress are stored only on your device, and we never see them.

PURCHASES

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active. See Apple's privacy policy.

GAME CENTER

Where an app uses Game Center for leaderboards or achievements, that service is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in Settings.

WHAT APPLE MAY SHARE WITH US

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymized crash reports and aggregate statistics, such as download counts. They don't identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs and understand how the apps are doing.

IF YOU EMAIL US

We use your address only to reply, and we don't share it.

CHANGES

If an app ever needs to collect anything, this page will be updated to say so before that app ships.
