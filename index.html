Arami Studios

A little piece of sky.

A small studio making simple, friendly apps.

THE NAME

The name started as a family story. In Guaraní, a language spoken in Paraguay, arami means "little sky." It seemed like the right size for what we want to make.

WHAT WE MAKE

Apps that are easy to pick up and pleasant to use. Many borrow the pixel look of the old 8-bit consoles. Our first one is in the works.
